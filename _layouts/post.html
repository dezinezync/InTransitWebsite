The text goes through the `platformTags` filter (.eleventy.js), which turns `macOS` and the other platform names in code elements into tags. The feeds carry the text as it is. The banner is _includes/post-banner.html. The styles of the page are in _sass/site/_blog.scss, and the styles of the text are in _sass/site/_prose.scss. {%- endcomment -%}
