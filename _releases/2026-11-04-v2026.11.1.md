### Improved

- The app now gets its list of interchanges from InTransit's servers. Before, it found an interchange only when two stations had the same name and were close together.
- When a trip runs on two lines, the route now shows each line and a "Change to" step at the interchange. Before, it could show the trip as one train. For example, in Ahmedabad, a trip from Old High Court to GIFT City changes at Motera Stadium and at GNLU.
