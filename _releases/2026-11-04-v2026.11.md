### New

- InTransit has a new look. One map and one sheet now cover each step of your trip: Explore, Station, Plan, Ready, In transit, and Arrived. Explore filters stations by line, and Settings, Travelogue, and your pass sit in one group of buttons on the map.
- Travelogue shows your trips on a shelf of 3D miniatures. You can now log out below your recent trips, and your trips stay on your device.
- When you start a trip, the map zooms in, tilts, and follows the direction you face. It keeps that zoom when you pull the sheet up or down, and it is flat and points north again after the trip.
- The In transit screen gives your route more room. The header shows your next stop, your next change, and your arrival in clearer type.
- A new "Change destination" row under the route lets you ride on to a later stop.
- Stations that are not in service show as gray markers that you can't select. They don't count as stops in your journey or in the Live Activity.

### Improved

- Line colours now match the line badges on the map, in Travelogue, on share cards, and in Change destination. Yellow lines use dark yellow text.
- All sheets use the glass of the system, and From and To show in one row each.
- The Live Activity is easier to read on the Lock Screen, and the train on the track is now a location pin in the colour of the line.
- The expanded Dynamic Island no longer shows a card with a black border, and its countdown is no longer cut off. Onboarding shows the new Dynamic Island for trips in Pune, Delhi, and Bengaluru.
- At the largest text sizes, the Ready sheet, the route, and the trip bar no longer break words or numbers.
- The trip panel and the share card take their tint from the map under them, and metro lines are smoother at bends.
- iPhone Duo: Onboarding, the trip screen, and the In transit bar follow the status bar at the side of the display. On the unfolded display held sideways, Change destination and Settings open as a panel at the leading edge.
- Pune: Civil Court is now District Court. Routes match the station by either name.
