### New

- Live tracking works much better underground and in other places with poor signal. Your progress, next station, and arrival stay correct through longer stretches without GPS, and the motion sensors of your iPhone keep boarding detection and your place on the map working.
- An estimated position marker shows on the map, with a dashed ring, when GPS drops out completely.
- Station search groups results by metro line, sorted by distance, with a filter bar for lines and quick picks for your Home and Work stations.
- A refreshed look for the pre-boarding screen, the navigation bar, the bottom sheet, and station details, with better contrast, larger-text support, and VoiceOver labels throughout.
- You can now go back from the From/To station confirmation screen.
- Onboarding has clearer messages, full support for larger text, and smoother animations when Reduce Motion is on.

### Improved

- InTransit tells you when background tracking can't stay on because "Always Allow" location access isn't granted, so you can fix it before your next trip.
- The Travelogue is one screen: your trips, distance, time, lines ridden, badges, and environmental impact, with a new "See All Trips" screen and a route thumbnail for each trip.
- The colours and style of the Live Activity are refined, and a display issue on Apple Watch is fixed.
- Live location tracking in the background is more reliable while you navigate a route.
- InTransit asks for "Always Allow" location access only when you start navigating a route.

### Fixed

- Fixed eco stats (CO₂, energy, and trees saved) that showed numbers far too high, and a bug where the app could miss your arrival on routes with a walk to or from the station.
- Fixed the Start button staying disabled when boarding at a station with poor GPS reception.
- Fixed two crashes, one when opening the search filter bar and one when loading saved app data, and a bug where filter buttons could trigger without a tap.
