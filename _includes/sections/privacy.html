Private by Design

Where You Go Is Your Business

InTransit uses your location on your iPhone to follow your ride. It never sends where you are to InTransit or anyone else.

  • Location stays on your iPhone. Your next station and arrival are worked out on the device.

  • Nothing that names you. No account needed to plan and ride. No tracking, no ads.

  • Asks only when it needs to. “Always Allow” comes up only when you start a ride.
