{%- comment -%} The body of a releases page. It shows the releases of one year. Two pages use this include: _pages/releases.html The newest year, at /releases/. _pages/releases-year.html One page for each year, at /releases//. The page must set `yearData` before it includes this file. `yearData` is one entry of `collections.releaseYears` (see .eleventy.js). The year switcher is a list of links, so it works without JavaScript. The script below the list replaces the list with a dropdown. The styles are in _sass/site/_releases.scss and _sass/site/_typography.scss (the header and the platform tags). Two filters of .eleventy.js change the notes of a release. `releaseNotes` colours the labels (New, Fixed, ...). `platformTags` turns `macOS` and the other platform names in code elements into tags. The feeds (releases.xml and releases.json) do not use these filters. {%- endcomment -%}
