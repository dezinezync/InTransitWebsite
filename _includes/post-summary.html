{%- comment -%} One item of a list of posts: the title, the date, and the first 50 words of the post. The blog index (_layouts/blog.html) and the pages of the categories (_layouts/category.html) use it inside a `ul.mt-post-list`. Use it with `render`, which gives the partial its own variables: {% render "post-summary.html", post: post, banner: true, first: forloop.first %} Parameters: post An entry of a collection of posts. banner Optional. true: shows the banner of the post above the title. The blog index shows the banners. The pages of the categories do not. first Optional. true: the item is the first of the list. Its banner loads at once, because it is at the top of the page. The excerpt is plain text. `strip_html` removes the tags first, and then `truncatewords` cuts the text after 50 words. A cut of the HTML itself could split a tag or a footnote marker. The styles are in _sass/site/_blog.scss. {%- endcomment -%}
