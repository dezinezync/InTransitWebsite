One Sentence

InTransit is a metro app for iPhone that follows your ride station by station in nine Indian cities, made by an indie developer in India.

Short Paragraph

InTransit is a metro app for iPhone that covers every running and under-construction line in Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai, Nagpur, New Delhi, and Pune. A Live Activity keeps the next station, the interchange, and the arrival time on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and InTransit sends a notification and a haptic tap when the rider’s stop comes up, even underground. Its Travelogue counts the distance ridden and the CO₂ saved, and unlocks 3D models of stations at distance milestones. The first five trips are free, and Weekly, Monthly, and Annual passes keep every ride feature.

Long Paragraph

InTransit is a metro app for iPhone that rides along with you. It covers every running and under-construction metro line in Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai, Nagpur, New Delhi, and Pune, and opens on the city you are in. Search groups stations by line and sorts them by distance, with Home and Work one tap away, and each station shows its lifts, ramps, and platform access. During a ride, a Live Activity keeps the next station, the interchange, and the arrival time on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, and in the Smart Stack on Apple Watch, and InTransit sends a notification and a haptic tap when the stop comes up. When GPS drops out in a tunnel, the motion sensors of the iPhone keep the ride on track, and maps and routes work with no signal. The Travelogue records each trip, the distance ridden, and the CO₂ saved, unlocks 3D models of stations at distance milestones, and shares a journey as an image or a video. InTransit works out the ride on the device and never sends the rider’s location away. The first five trips are free; Weekly, Monthly, and Annual passes keep every ride feature, and the Travelogue stays open without one. InTransit is made by Nikhil Nigade, an indie developer in India, and has been on the App Store since March 2026.
